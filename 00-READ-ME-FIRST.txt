RIFTLINE - APEX PERFORMANCE SUITE
=================================

Version 23 opens on the settings Apex is actually using, so Apply only changes
what you changed. Review shows every change as old -> new with one-click undo,
Launch asks before leaving changes unapplied, and Steam is found on any drive.
It also fixes the texture-budget values, FOV math, and Ragdoll collision switch.
See README.txt for the full list.

1. Extract the entire ZIP first.
2. Close Apex Legends.
3. Double-click Riftline.exe. START-HERE.cmd remains as a fallback.
4. Choose a profile or tune settings, then review and apply.
5. Click Launch Apex when you are ready.

Riftline does not start a localhost web server and does not need an internet
connection for its interface or settings engine. It loads its packaged UI files
directly inside a native Windows window. Steam/Apex still need their normal
connection when you actually launch the game.

To add Riftline to your desktop, double-click CREATE-DESKTOP-SHORTCUT.cmd.

That is all most people need.

Display now includes a live aspect-ratio geometry lab. The circle remains round
with black bars and becomes an ellipse when a non-native aspect ratio is
stretched to remove the bars. Visuals and Advanced use all 38 supplied Apex
captures and switch the preview to the exact setting you focus or change.

Launch Studio now includes gameplay and lobby FPS caps, FPS counters, intro,
controller, V-Sync, priority, developer, CFG, language, window-mode, adaptive
scale, and expert command controls. Hover any command card for a plain-language
description. Advanced includes FOV choices through 120. The Reticle Designer
adds a live color wheel, RGB fields, color swatches, backup, and profile saving.

Resolution + FPS, Extra FPS, Launch, and Undo + Help are optional. Extra FPS
includes Most FPS, Balanced, and Best Picture presets. Those presets do not change
your selected output resolution.

For the final safe performance boost, use Best FPS. It now sets Geometry LOD
and Object/Effect Fade Distance to 0.25 and Particle Fallback to Aggressive.
Forced grass, smoke, or player-effect removal is intentionally not included.

Advanced FOV now ranges from 70 through 120. Values above Apex's normal menu
range can still be clamped by a future game build. Lower FOV may give a small
extra FPS gain but narrows your view.

Use RESTORE REGULAR APEX on Easy Setup to undo all applied helper settings and
clear its launch commands. Your named custom profiles are kept.

Resolution + FPS has grouped resolution presets for 4:3, 5:4,
16:9, 16:10, 3:2, and ultrawide presets.

On Display, choose No black bars.
When you click Apply changes, the app automatically forces Fullscreen and
saves the Apex letterbox commands with your selected width and height. You do not
need to paste anything into Steam or change your Windows/monitor resolution.
Choose Black bars to remove the helper-managed stretch commands.

If Windows asks whether to run the file, confirm only if this is the ZIP you
received from Codex. The app never modifies Apex's installed game files.
